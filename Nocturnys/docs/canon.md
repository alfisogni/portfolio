\# Spatial Canon



The Observatory is the first destination.



The Observatory introduces direction and possibility.



The Lake is the central hub of Nocturnys.



The Lake connects all major spaces.



The Workshop represents creation.



The Library represents memory and influence.



The Waterfall is accessed from the Lake.



The Waterfall is a transition, not a destination.



The Heart lies beneath the Waterfall.



The Heart is the deepest accessible space in Nocturnys.



The visitor should naturally encounter the Workshop and Library before descending toward the Heart.
